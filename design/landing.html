Why Porygon
Everything a whiteboard needs — plus a pulse
All the free-form drawing of Excalidraw, with a simulation layer that turns a static diagram into a living system you can stress-test.
✎

Full Excalidraw canvas

Infinite canvas, freehand drawing, bound arrows, live multiplayer collaboration, and instant PNG/SVG export — the whole drawing toolkit you already know.

▣

Real system-design icons

Load balancers, SQL & NoSQL databases, caches, queues, CDNs, and cloud services — a complete component library, inspired by Eraser.io's icon set.

⚡

Traffic & failure simulation

Inject traffic spikes, kill a node, flush a cache, or partition the network — then watch latency, errors and queue depth ripple through your design live.

▤

Guided practice scenarios

A library of real interview prompts — URL shortener, rate limiter, news feed, ride dispatch — each with a rubric so you know what "good" looks like.

◷

Live health scoring

Every node gets a health state — healthy, strained, overloaded — plus automatic single-point-of-failure detection as your design runs under load.

⇄

Share & review together

Send a link, run a mock interview, or drop comments pinned right on the node that's about to fall over.
